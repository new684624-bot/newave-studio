Privacy Policy — Newave Studio

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 | Brand: Newave Studio (thenewavestudio.vercel.app)

Newave Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates the official storefront showcase at thenewavestudio.vercel.app and internal content publishing tools for our official social media channels (including Pinterest, Instagram, and TikTok).

1. Information We Collect

Our website is a static brand catalog linking to our official authorized retail partners (Spreadshirt and Zazzle). We do not require visitors to register accounts, nor do we collect, store, or process personal payment or shipping information directly on this domain.

2. Internal API & Social Media Integration (Pinterest & Meta APIs)

Our internal publishing application connects strictly to Newave Studio's own authenticated business accounts on Pinterest and Instagram to schedule and publish original product pins, lookbooks, and short-form videos, and to read performance metrics for our own boards and posts.

  • We do not access, collect, store, or share personal data from third-party Pinterest or Instagram users.
  • All API access tokens are stored securely in encrypted local environment variables and are never shared with third parties.

3. Third-Party Marketplaces

When you click product links to purchase Newave Studio merchandise on Zazzle or Spreadshirt, your transaction and personal data are governed by the respective privacy policies of Zazzle Inc. and Spreadshirt (sprd.net AG).

4. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Because we do not collect or store personal user data, no personal user records are retained on our servers. For any privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, please contact us via our official Instagram channel @thenewave.studio or Pinterest @newavestudio.
